How to Remove an Image Background Online

BGErase lets you remove an image background directly in a modern web browser. The process is designed to be simple on phones, tablets and desktops.

Step 1: Upload your image

Open the BGErase home page and tap Choose Image, or drag an image into the upload box. You can also paste an image from your clipboard on supported browsers.

Step 2: Wait while the background is removed

After upload, the browser loads the AI model and creates a foreground mask. A progress indicator shows the current stage.

Step 3: Check the preview

The result preview uses a checkerboard pattern to make transparent areas easy to see. Look around hair, glasses, clothing, hands and other fine edges before downloading.

Step 4: Download transparent PNG

Tap Download Transparent PNG. PNG is used because it can store transparent pixels.

Tips for better results

  • Use a clear, reasonably sharp image.
  • Keep the main subject separated from the background when possible.
  • Avoid very dark clothing against a very dark background when you need perfect edges.
  • Check the preview before using the cutout in a design.
Privacy: BGErase performs the removal in the browser rather than uploading your selected photo to a BGErase application server.
