About BGErase

BGErase is a lightweight web project focused on making background removal and everyday image editing easier from a phone or computer.

Our goal

The goal is a clear interface: upload an image, understand what is happening, inspect the result and download the output without unnecessary steps.

Privacy approach

The main background-removal workflow uses a secure image-processing service to create the transparent result. Uploaded images are used only for the requested transformation.

Important limitation

Automated segmentation is not perfect for every possible image. Fine hair, reflections, smoke, transparent objects and foreground/background colors that look very similar can produce imperfect masks.
