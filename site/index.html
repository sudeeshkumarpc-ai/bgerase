Frequently Asked Questions

Is BGErase free?

The website is designed as a free online background remover with no watermark added by BGErase.

What image formats can I upload?

Common formats such as JPG, JPEG, PNG and WebP are supported, along with other image formats that your browser can decode.

Will my image be uploaded to a BGErase server?

The background-removal service processes your selected image securely to create the transparent result. BGErase does not use your image for advertising.

Why can an AI cutout sometimes be imperfect?

Very similar foreground and background colors, heavy overlap, transparent objects, smoke, reflections and extremely fine details can challenge automated segmentation. Always check the preview. If the AI removes part of the subject, use Refine Result to restore it before downloading.

What do I get after removal?

You get a PNG with transparency so the removed area can be placed over another background.
