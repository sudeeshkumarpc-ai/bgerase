Privacy Policy

What BGErase does

BGErase provides browser-based image background removal and related image tools.

Image processing

The main background-removal feature processes the selected image in your browser. BGErase does not require your image to be uploaded to a BGErase application server for the removal operation.

Model resources

The browser may download AI model and runtime resources from the configured third-party content delivery service. These resources are used to perform the processing locally in the browser.

Information you provide

If you contact BGErase by email, the information you include in your message is used to respond to your request.

Cookies and analytics

This starter version does not intentionally add advertising trackers or a user account system.

Third-party services

Browser model resources and hosting infrastructure may be provided by third parties. Their own policies may apply to those requests.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the website's functionality changes. The updated date will be shown at the top of this page.
